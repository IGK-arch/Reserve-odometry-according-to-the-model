# GNSS and navigation round 2 evidence

`heading_fix_snapshot.json` and `heading_fix_train_validation.json` are the final navigation-only study: independent antenna-pair integrity guard, physical body heading, and consistent decaying startup heading for yaw and the position lever arm. The numerical speed estimator is the frozen round-1 baseline, so these results isolate navigation. Final integrated Estimator and ROS evidence is separate.

The final comparison uses all 33 train and 13 validation representatives in four GNSS schedules. Every scenario preserves wheel velocity and integrated wheel distance exactly, along with publication stamps and absolute-position masks. Two short train recordings lie over 500 m off the route map and correctly retain relative odometry; they are reported as missing absolute coverage, with null absolute RMSE. They are never treated as zero-error poses.

Sparse GNSS validation improves on all 13 recordings: pooled 3D proxy RMSE 1.884447 to 1.840987 m, median 1.497161 to 1.461775 m. Frozen-GNSS pooled validation improves 1.795451 to 1.752567 m. First-30-seconds-only and delayed-GNSS validation regress in pooled RMSE, 2.664054 to 2.798108 m. `startup_anchor_geometry_diagnostic.json` explains how correcting a roughly positive 0.8 m rover-map bias removes accidental compensation for later wheel/map error during a long blackout. This limitation is retained explicitly.

The independent stationary curve regression failed before the lever-arm fix by 3.93372 m and passes afterward at numerical precision. It also checks heading decay, rigid length, matching/master-anchor isolation, wheel-state isolation, and grade. All 13 integrated C++ executables pass; the new standalone regression passes ASan/UBSan. The reviewed runtime Navigation SHA-256 is `611d02032aaa2bf3c1beced8a9fd6c0edc40890f6c69b824023add210cdda571`.

The public fused-reference diagnostic is post-selection only: 1.566332 to 1.474923 m 3D RMSE. The internally inconsistent intermediate geometry version scored 1.452326 m; its better aggregate score was not used to retain a physically inconsistent yaw/position transform. The stationary recording diagnostic improves from 1.204196 m originally and 2.399469 m in that intermediate version to 0.113814 m after the independent fix.

Earlier files remain as historical experiments. `study_summary.json` describes pair-only selection; `rejected_uncertainty_train.json` records a discarded variance gate that blocked genuine GNSS drift recovery. `geometry_snapshot.json` and `geometry_pair_train_validation.json` precede the startup lever-arm correction. `selected_pair_guard.patch`, `rejected_uncertainty_gate.patch`, and `startup_heading_consistency.patch` preserve the exact experiment changes. Runtime binaries and frozen build sources are identified by SHA-256; generic ablation workspace hashes are evaluation context and are not substituted for the actual snapshot sources.

To reproduce comparisons from the preserved baseline CSV cache, run `evaluation/navigation_gnss_candidate_study.py` with `--baseline-dir`, a diagnostic-only rebuilt `--baseline-exe`, the candidate `--exe`, a new `--outdir`, and `--split train validation`. The script refuses changed original baseline fields or unequal absolute-position masks. It never passes scoring proxy or fused reference positions to runtime.
